## 🌱 About the Project

**Less Plastic, More Life** is an environmental awareness and advocacy project that aims to raise awareness about single-use plastic pollution and encourage individuals to adopt reusable and sustainable habits.

The project highlights how everyday plastic consumption affects wildlife, waterways, communities, and the environment.

Through simple actions and individual commitments, everyone can contribute to creating a cleaner and healthier planet.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Raise awareness about single-use plastic pollution.
- Encourage the use of reusable alternatives.
- Promote proper waste segregation and recycling.
- Inspire individuals to practice sustainable habits.
- Encourage community participation in environmental protection.

## 🌍 Why It Matters

Plastic pollution is a growing environmental concern that affects both ecosystems and human communities.

### 🌱 It Rarely Goes Away
Plastic can break down into smaller pieces called microplastics and remain in the environment for a long time.

### 🐢 It Harms Wildlife
Marine animals and birds may mistake plastic for food or become trapped in plastic waste.

### 💧 It Affects Communities
Improperly discarded plastic can pollute waterways and contribute to clogged drainage systems and environmental contamination.

## 👩‍⚕️ Connection to Nursing

As a nursing student, understanding environmental health is essential because human health is closely connected to the environment.

Plastic pollution can affect water quality, food sources, sanitation, and the overall health of communities.

Promoting environmental responsibility is also a way of promoting disease prevention, health education, and community well-being.

## 💭 Project Reflection

This advocacy project taught me that health goes beyond the hospital. As a nursing student, I recognize that plastic pollution affects people too. It contaminates water and food sources and can contribute to health problems in communities.

This activity also reflects the Ateneo values. **Magis** encouraged me to use my skills in technology and design to do more than the minimum. **Cura personalis** reminded me that caring for people includes caring for the environment they live in. The idea of being a person for and with others inspired me to create a space where people can learn and take a pledge.

I also learned that AI tools can help me build a website quickly, but I still need to think critically, choose a cause that matters, and ensure that my content is accurate.

In the future, I want to use my nursing profession and digital skills to promote health education and protect our community and planet.
