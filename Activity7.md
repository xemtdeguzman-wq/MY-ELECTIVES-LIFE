## 🩷 CLINICAL EXPERIENCE

My clinical exposure has provided opportunities to participate in different areas of nursing care under clinical supervision.

### Operating Room
- Served as scrub and circulating student nurse during cesarean section, thyroidectomy, exploratory laparotomy, salpingectomy, and oophorectomy.
- Maintained surgical asepsis and assisted with instrument preparation, surgical counts, specimen handling, and perioperative care.

### OB-ER and IE Room
- Assisted in patient triage and initial assessments.
- Performed IV cannulation and IV fluid regulation.

### Medical-Surgical and Other Clinical Areas
- Provided oxygen therapy and assisted during Code Blue emergencies.
- Participated in CPR, chest compressions, and bag-valve-mask ventilation under supervision.
- Delivered nursing care to geriatric and psychiatric patients.
- Conducted patient assessments and monitored vital signs.
- Prepared and administered prescribed medications under supervision.
- Performed medication calculations, NGT feeding, and capillary blood glucose monitoring.
- Developed Nursing Care Plans and documented interventions using FDAR.

### Community Health
- Participated in community health assessments and home visits.
- Assisted in preventive health education activities.
